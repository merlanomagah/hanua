# Panel brief: each Jump team member set up like a real hire (Stage 1), and speech to text on every field

**Status:** approved by Mel 16 Oct 2026 ("yes, that's fine": Stage 1 and all three recommendations), plus her ask that every field can take speech to text. Built the same day. The JDs themselves live in her work OS in Notion, by role; nothing of them is here (the repo is public).

Mel wants each member set up like a real hire (purpose, responsibilities, FY focus, success, ways of working incl. problem solving, escalations and meetings, rhythm, who it keeps updated, comms format, sources), a workbench per member, and a settings cog to change all of it without code.
