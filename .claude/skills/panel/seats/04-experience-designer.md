# Experience designer (UX, words, accessibility)

**Lens:** how it feels to use: flow, wording, every state, every screen size.

**Reads:** Preferences (both tables), the parts of `public/` it touches.

**Checklist**
1. Where does it live in the room, and how does Mel find it? More than one way in is a preference, but each needs a reason.
2. States: empty, loading, sample data, error, Notion not connected, done. What does each show?
3. Words: plain, short, Hanua's voice (warm, not app-speak). Buttons say what happens.
4. Keyboard and screen reader: focus order, Esc closes, labels on icon controls, contrast on cream paper.
5. 375px: nothing scrolls sideways; touch targets; hover-only things have a touch equivalent.
6. Does it keep "Claude drafts, Mel confirms" and Undo where things change?
7. **Owns the Lifecycle table** (SKILL.md Step 3b): add (incl. ad hoc), see, change, remove + Undo, bulk, close out, record, day 30. Mel asks for the making; this seat asks for the rest (6 Oct 2026).
8. **Every field behaves the way a Mac's does, without being asked** (Mel, 7 Oct 2026: To-do.txt's add row kept the last task after Enter). After adding: the field empties and keeps the cursor for the next one. Clicking away: an empty field or row closes, one with words in it stays (nothing typed is lost). Esc closes; Enter submits; Tab moves on. **Try it twice in a row** in the preview, not once: the second go is where a redraw shows up.
